Common questions

How do I add a movie from a clip?
Tap Share on a TikTok, Instagram Reel, or YouTube Short and choose MovieBin, or paste the link with “Add from a clip”. MovieBin keeps working on the clip in the background; it appears in the clip dock above the tab bar until you confirm the film.

How do I remove ads?
Subscribe to MovieBin Plus in Profile → “Go ad-free with Plus”. Use “Restore purchases” on a new device.

How do I cancel MovieBin Plus?
iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Deleting your MovieBin account does not cancel a store subscription.

How do I delete my account?
In the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your watchlist, groups and clip history. You can also email us to request deletion.
