Who is responsible

MovieBin is published by Ben Blankenburg (“we”). Contact: ben.blankenburg@icloud.com.

What we process and why

  • Your account. Email address, and the name Apple or Google share with us if you sign in with them. Used to create and secure your account (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, performance of contract).
  • Your library. The films you save, watched and favourite flags, your movie groups, and the clip links you share or paste, with the matches we found for them. Used to provide the app (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Clip identification. When you add a clip, we send its link to our data provider (Bright Data) to read the clip’s public caption, description and comments. If those do not name a film clearly, the public text is sent to a text model through OpenRouter. Candidate titles are checked against The Movie Database (TMDB). We do not download or analyse the video, and we send no account details to these providers. Public results are cached for up to 30 days so the same clip is not fetched twice.
  • Security and abuse prevention. Request counters per account and technical logs (Art. 6(1)(f), legitimate interest in a secure, affordable service).
  • Advertising. If you do not have MovieBin Plus, the app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may process device identifiers (on iPhone only if you allow tracking), IP address, coarse location and ad interactions. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google’s consent form before any personalised ads (Art. 6(1)(a)); without consent you see non-personalised ads. You can change your choice any time in Profile → Ad privacy choices. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • App usage analytics. To see which features are used and fix what does not work, the app records usage events with PostHog, hosted in the EU (Frankfurt): app opens, screens viewed and actions such as adding a film, sharing a clip or starting a purchase, with your device model, OS and app version, linked to your MovieBin account ID. We do not record your email, search text or which films you watch, and we do not use this data for advertising or share it with others. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f)). You can turn it off any time in Profile → Share usage data.
  • Purchases. MovieBin Plus is sold through the App Store or Google Play; Apple or Google process the payment. We use RevenueCat to check your subscription status, linked to your MovieBin account ID. We never see your payment details.

Service providers

Supabase (database, authentication and server functions; hosted in Frankfurt, Germany), Bright Data, OpenRouter and the model provider it routes to, TMDB, Google (AdMob, Google Sign-In), Apple (Sign in with Apple, App Store), RevenueCat, and PostHog (app usage analytics; EU hosting in Frankfurt, Germany). Some of these providers are located in the United States; transfers rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep data

Account and library data are kept until you delete your account. Clip links in your inbox are shown for 14 days. Request counters are deleted when their time window ends. Deleting your account (Profile → Delete account) permanently erases your account, library, groups and clip history from our database.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict and port your data, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. Email us to exercise these rights. You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority.

Children

MovieBin is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will update this page when our processing changes and show the new effective date above.
